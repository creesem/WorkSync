# WorkSync

WorkSync is the local-first work-management platform over the WorkSync Obsidian
vault, currently being reworked as a merged repository (wayfinder effort
`worksync-platform-rework`).

This is the **seed** glossary. The full merged vocabulary is produced by wayfinder
ticket 02, which merges the source `CONTEXT.md` (project, evidence authority,
retrieval) with the operator documents `70`/`71` (project, person, ticket, meeting,
action). Until then, do not coin new domain terms here.

## Authority

- The Obsidian vault is the authoritative work record. It is never moved by this project.
- AI and heuristic results are suggestions, never auto-written truth.
- Canonical note state wins over any generated view.
